v1 · the first 70 days

92 posts from 17 Sep to 1 Nov (2.0 a day), then nothing for 24 days. They reached 2,399 people and were played 3,275 times — a median of 12 people per post on day one. Reels reach 2.8× further than carousels, but a reel was watched for 3.9s of ~20 on average and the whole run earned 8 shares and 1 save. When posting stopped, reach went to zero.

What it taught: the formats were not stopping the scroll, the jokes leaned sharp rather than funny, and with no new posts there is no reach at all. v2 is built on those three.
